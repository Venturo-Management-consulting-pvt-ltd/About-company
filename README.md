# Venturo Management Consulting Pvt. Ltd.

**Operational Transformation • Downstream Supply Chain Design • Sustainable Rural & Ecological Systems**

Founded in 2014 and headquartered in Doddaballapura, Karnataka, **Venturo Management Consulting** bridges rigorous operational frameworks with ground-level execution. We partner with enterprises, government bodies, and grassroots producer networks to identify and eliminate systemic bottlenecks across supply chains, manufacturing, and rural ecosystems.

### What We Do
* **Theory of Constraints (TOC) & Supply Chain Engineering:** Designing pull-based distribution, inventory replenishment, and downstream retail research frameworks that improve throughput and working capital efficiency.
* **Grassroots Livelihood & Rural Value Chains:** Structuring scalable production and van-sales distribution models for women-led producer enterprises and rural livelihood missions.
* **Ecological Engineering & Agro-Forestry:** Developing bamboo-based bio-drainage and phytoremediation solutions for wastewater management, alongside scientific cultivation and evaluation of high-value agro-forestry assets.
* **Digital & Data Tooling:** Building lightweight web platforms, field-audit tools, and analytical workflows to support operational visibility.
